PRISM: Prospect Role Identification & Scouting Model
Basketball Analytics · Multi-Stage Predictive Architecture
Multi-stage draft projection system trained on rolling historical cohorts to predict 5-year NBA EPM and rotation probability. Integrates role-aware feature engineering, play-type efficiency splits, contextual production normalization, cross-era percentile scaling, and stability-adjusted priors. Employs pairwise gradient boosting for relative ranking calibration and leakage-controlled training windows to preserve forward validity. Designed to identify structural mispricings between consensus evaluation and long-term impact.
ELWAY: Team Projection Model
Football Analytics and Modeling
Quarterback-adjusted NFL Elo framework incorporating QB VALUE ratings, injury simulations, opponent adjustments, and reproducible data pipelines for weekly probabilistic forecasts and playoff simulations.
QBERT: Quarterback Rating Model
Football Analytics and Modeling
