Research Projects

I work in quantitative sports analytics, helping build statistical models across professional basketball, football, and collegiate athletics. My work blends modeling, large-scale data engineering, and longform writing aimed at making complex ideas intuitive.

I also collaborate with a professional basketball organization, developing proprietary metrics, tracking systems, and scouting tools to support player evaluation and decision-making. My research interests include advantage creation, value estimation, and optimizing under real-world constraints.

In addition to sports analytics, I conduct labor-market research using large-scale skills and employment data to study how technology and employer behavior shape economic opportunity.

My academic background spans computer science, statistics, and applied modeling. I’m especially interested in how human behavior interacts with statistical systems.

Selected writing and projects are available throughout this site.
